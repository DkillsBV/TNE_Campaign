If a small party is wiped out, an NPC rescuer saves them, at a price.

How I use it here: the original rule gives each character a minor lasting stat penalty and makes them owe the rescuer a favour. I use only the favour, not the penalty. In the first battle the party is captured by [[Vashtera Aupeth III]] and let go, and the debt is the story, not a stat loss. See [[Encounter - Vashtera's finishing blow]].

Source: Stolen Rules, volume 1, by ShawnsVTTs, rule 06.8.
