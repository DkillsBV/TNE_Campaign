When the leader falls or half of a group is down, the rest check morale. Most of them run or surrender. Fights end when they should, and not when the last soldier dies.

Used by the army: if an [[Imperial Sergeant]] dies, nearby [[Imperial Conscript]] must succeed on a DC 10 Wisdom save or flee. The veteran Unton line under [[Commander Torgrim Ashvein]] is the exception and holds.

Source: Stolen Rules, volume 1, by ShawnsVTTs, rule 11.4.
