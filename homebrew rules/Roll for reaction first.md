Before anyone rolls initiative, the DM rolls 2d6 to see how the monsters feel about the party.
- 2 to 5: hostile.
- 6 to 8: unsure, and open to talk.
- 9 to 12: not looking for a fight.

A good Charisma modifier can push the roll. Use it for any encounter that isn't already a fight, such as the road encounters in [[Road Encounters The 5000 km Journey]].

Source: Stolen Rules, volume 1, by ShawnsVTTs, rule 01.2.
