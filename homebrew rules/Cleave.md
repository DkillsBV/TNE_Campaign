When an attack drops a creature to 0 HP, the leftover damage carries to another creature within reach, and it can chain.

Notes for the 100-soldier battle:
- Against 1 HP soldiers, a hit that deals 7 damage can in theory kill several of them. If that gets out of hand, cap it at 2 or 3 extra targets per attack. This cap is my suggestion and not part of the original rule.
- Only melee attacks cleave.

Source: Stolen Rules, volume 1, by ShawnsVTTs, rule 04.10.
