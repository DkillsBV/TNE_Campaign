Run big groups of enemies as groups, and keep the boss separate.

- Roll one initiative per group of enemies. A boss rolls its own.
- A group acts all at once on its turn.
- The DM says which creature attacks whom, and gives the attack bonus and the damage die. The players roll the hit on themselves, so they stay involved.

Source: Stolen Rules, volume 1, by ShawnsVTTs, rule 04.6.
