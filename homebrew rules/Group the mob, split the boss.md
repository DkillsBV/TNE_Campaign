Run big groups of enemies as groups, and keep the boss separate.

- Roll one initiative per group of enemies. A boss rolls its own.
- A group acts all at once on its turn.
- You say which soldier attacks whom, and give the attack bonus and the damage die. The players roll the hit on themselves, so they stay involved.

Use it for the army in [[Encounter - Vashtera's finishing blow]]: each group of [[Imperial Conscript]] and [[Imperial Shieldbearer]] is one initiative, and each [[Imperial Sergeant]] leads one group.

Source: Stolen Rules, volume 1, by ShawnsVTTs, rule 04.6.
