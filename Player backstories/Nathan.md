Kairos Crimson is a Dragonborn who grew up in the Crimson Forests, his beloved home. When a powerful demon began destroying the forest, Kairos made a pact with another demon, gaining dark magical powers. With these newlyfound powers, he defeated the demon and returned home.

But victory came too late. Most of his people were already gone, their once-bright crimson eyes having lost their light. Seeing what remained of his home broke something inside Kairos.

Now he has left the Crimson Forests once more. He hunts the demons responsible for the suffering of his people, seeking revenge and justice. He will fight to build a world where no one has to endure the same fate he did.
