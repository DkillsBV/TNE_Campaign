Human army of the [[Empire of Am Aupeth]] · Minion · Melee only · Homebrew (new enemy)

Fodder like a [[Imperial Conscript]], but harder to hit. They hold the front of the line.

**Imperial Shieldbearer**
Medium Humanoid (Human), Lawful Neutral

- **AC** 15 (shield, gambeson)
- **HP** 1
- **Speed** 30 ft

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 9 (-1) | 10 (+0) | 8 (-1) |

- **Senses** passive Perception 10
- **Languages** Common
- **CR** 1/4 (50 XP) · **Proficiency Bonus** +2

**Traits**
- **Fodder.** Any damage reduces the Shieldbearer to 0 HP. A missed attack, or a successful save against an effect that deals damage, does nothing to it.
- **Shield Wall.** While the Shieldbearer is within 5 ft of another Shieldbearer, both have +1 AC.

**Actions**
- **Mace.** Melee Attack Roll: +3, reach 5 ft. Hit: 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage.
- **Shield Bash (Recharge 6).** Melee Attack Roll: +3, reach 5 ft. Hit: 3 (1d4 + 1) bludgeoning damage, and the target is pushed 5 ft away from the Shieldbearer.

## Notes

- Use them to block a doorway, a road or a bridge. The shove is for positioning, not damage.
- A Shieldbearer alone is not a threat. Put three or four in a line with Conscripts behind them.
