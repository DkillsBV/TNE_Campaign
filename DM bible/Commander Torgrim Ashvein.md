Commander of the [[Unton]] forces at Dunmarch Hold · Does not fight · The party talks to him · Homebrew (new NPC)

Everything below is my proposal. Change anything that doesn't fit.

## Who he is

A male dwarf in his early 70s, which is young for a dwarf. He enlisted as soon as he came of age and has been fighting on the frontier ever since, so he looks and sounds older than he is: a dark beard with early grey, deep lines around the eyes and a scar or two. He has already done his time. He commands about 2,500 Unton soldiers at the border and has led the defence of Dunmarch Hold since the western province fell, which made him a commander far earlier than most. He has fought [[Empire of Am Aupeth]]'s armies several times and tried many different tactics against them. He is practised, tired and careful. Older officers defer to him because he has seen more fighting than they have, even though he is younger.

He does not take part in the fight. He directs the battle from the wall or a command post behind the line, and the party reaches him there before and after.

## How he talks

Short, flat sentences. He trusts people who show him something and not people who promise something. He has stopped being surprised by Am Aupeth and is not hopeful either. He speaks of the Motherhouse's soldiers as drilled and brave and wrong.

## What he wants from the party

- Information on what they have seen and heard. He needs anyone who has been inside the border.
- A job done at a point on the line where he can't spare soldiers. Treat this as a reason for the party to be in the thick of the fight.
- He doesn't order them. He asks, and says plainly it is a favour.

## What he knows

Use the adventure 1 clues from [[The Price of the First Crown]] as his rumours, and let him confirm the party's own findings:
- Villages across the border are empty with the tables still set.
- The fields in annexed land have never been this good.
- Am Aupeth soldiers fight harder than any conscript should.

He has no idea about Vaskor. He knows only that something at the heart of the empire is wrong.

## Before the fight

- He expects the attack and has set his line on practice, not on hope.
- If the party shares what they know, he adjusts his plan, and the information helps the Unton line hold longer.
- He tells the party that the empire has never sent its own ruler to a border fight. The soldiers do the work.

## During the fight

The party sees him giving orders from his post and changing the line as it shifts. He does not join, and a messenger comes and goes between him and the front. When Vashtera appears, he is the first to understand what it means, and he orders the retreat.

## After

- If the party wakes up in Dunmarch Hold, he is waiting.
- He says what he saw: an army that held, a woman who walked through it, and a party that fell. He is quiet about it.
- He tells them what Unton has lost, and asks what they plan to do next.

## Hooks for later

- He can give the party a letter or token that opens doors in Unton.
- He is a bridge to Unton's crown and to the other nations arming against Am Aupeth, like [[Kingdom of Taginumn]].
- If you want him to die later, let it be after the party has trusted him.

## Stats

He does not fight in this encounter, so he has no stat block. If he ever needs one, use an official 5.5e Veteran or Knight stat block.
