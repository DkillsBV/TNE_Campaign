The one that keeps [[Imperial Conscript]] and [[Imperial Shieldbearer]] in line. Still low damage.

**Imperial Sergeant**
Medium Humanoid (Human), Lawful Neutral

- **AC** 14 (scale shirt)
- **HP** 27 (5d8 + 5)
- **Speed** 30 ft

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 (+2) | 11 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 13 (+1) |

- **Senses** passive Perception 11
- **Languages** Common
- **CR** 1/2 (100 XP) · **Proficiency Bonus** +2

**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** The Sergeant makes two Sword attacks.
- **Sword.** Melee Attack Roll: +4, reach 5 ft. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing damage.
- **Hold the Line (Recharge 5-6, bonus action).** Up to three allies within 30 ft that can hear the Sergeant each gain 4 temporary hit points.

## Notes

- If the Sergeant falls, Conscripts that fail a DC 10 Wisdom save flee at the start of their turn.
- One Sergeant per 10 or so soldiers. More than that makes the group too strong for low-level players.
