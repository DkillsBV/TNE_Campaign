ONLY FOR THE DM · Secret lore behind [[Vashtera Aupeth III]] · Covers adventures 1 and 2

This is a proposal. Change anything that doesn't fit your world.

## The truth

Vashtera's power does not come from [[The First]] or any of the twelve. It comes from **[[Vaskor, the Stolen Crown]]**, a being with no seat, sealed by the Senate across the continents. The Dravethis seal is being opened, and the Am Aupeth empire is the channel through which he reaches out.

- **The scar:** the Motherhouse's records call the scar at the heart of the [[Empire of Am Aupeth]] "the price of the first crown". It is where the Dravethis seal sits. Long ago an entire population disappeared there in one year. Their lives were taken and they never reached [[Velenna, Seat of the Gentle Door]]'s door.
- **The payment:** the soil above the scar is unnaturally fertile because that is where the stolen life leaks out.
- **The Crown:** Vaskor's symbol is a crown with its jewels pried out. Only the Empress wears the **Stolen Crown**, and only she is touched by his power. The Handmaiden-Governors are ordinary officials without power. Whatever a province loses (the vanished, the pacified villages, the famine, the war dead) feeds him through her.
- **Vashtera's role:** she is the third Empress to serve him, and the first to widen it. "Reunification" feeds the seal's weakening and his power. Tieflings, halflings and gnomes who vanish near the scar are not lost. They are payment.

## Why she is cleric-like

She presents as a priestess of the Motherhouse and casts as a cleric, but her spells are borrowed life, not divine favour. Radiant on the surface, a hollow pull underneath. [[Morwenne, Seat of the Unlit Room]] governs dark and grief, so the Curtain-Keepers might notice something wrong. [[Ghestra, Seat of the Slow Rot]] reports to the Senate on decay that is being accelerated by interference, and this could be that.

## What she wants

To keep growing the empire, so that Vaskor is always fed and her line is never cut off. She spares the level-3 party because they are too weak to be worth feeding to it or fearing.

## Clues, adventure 1 (party level 1 to 3)

1. A refugee or trader says the border villages are not burning. They are simply empty, with tables set.
2. The harvest in annexed lands is the best in years, even where the people are gone.
3. A Handmaiden-Governor's officials speak of the Empress as if her word were already law, even before it is spoken.
4. Meeting Vashtera: she wins without effort, spares them, and says nothing about the source of her power.

## Clues, adventure 2 (party level 3 to 5)

1. The party finds the crown with the empty jewel settings in old paintings and carvings.
2. A defector or hidden record from the Motherhouse names "the price of the first crown" and the year of the vanishing.
3. Near the scar, spirits of the missing are stuck and cannot move on. A Doorkeeper of Velenna can confirm they never arrived.
4. The party learns the Stolen Crown is the link between the Empress and Vaskor.
