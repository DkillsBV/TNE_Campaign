ONLY FOR THE DM · Secret lore behind [[Vashtera Aupeth III]] · Covers adventures 1 and 2

This is a proposal. Change anything that doesn't fit your world.

## The truth

Vashtera's power does not come from [[The First]] or any of the twelve. The Motherhouse's records call the scar at the heart of the [[Empire of Am Aupeth]] "the price of the first crown". The truth is that the first Empress made a bargain with something that is not a god. It lives under the scar and is called **the Hollow**, a hunger that has no seat in the [[CONCORD HALL, Seat of the Senate]].

- **The price:** the first Empress gave it an entire population in one year. The people did not die in a way [[Velenna, Seat of the Gentle Door]] could guide. Their lives were swallowed whole, and she never meets them at her door.
- **The payment:** in return, the bloodline gets power, as long as the Hollow keeps being fed. The soil above it is unnaturally fertile because that is where the stolen life leaks out.
- **The Crown:** the Empress wears the **Hollow Crown**. Each Handmaiden-Governor wears a **crown shard**. The shards are conduits: whatever a province loses (the vanished, the pacified villages, the famine, the war dead) flows through them to the Empress.
- **Vashtera's role:** she is the third Empress to keep the bargain, and the first to widen it. "Reunification" is how she feeds it. Tieflings, halflings and gnomes who vanish near the scar are not lost. They are payment.

## Why she is cleric-like

She presents as a priestess of the Motherhouse and casts as a cleric, but her spells are borrowed life, not divine favour. Radiant on the surface, a hollow pull underneath. [[Morwenne, Seat of the Unlit Room]] governs dark and grief, so the Curtain-Keepers might notice something wrong. [[Ghestra, Seat of the Slow Rot]] reports to the Senate on decay that is being accelerated by interference, and this could be that.

## What she wants

To keep growing the empire, so that the Hollow is always fed and her line is never cut off. She spares the level-3 party because they are too weak to be worth feeding to it or fearing.

## Clues, adventure 1 (party level 1 to 3)

1. A refugee or trader says the border villages are not burning. They are simply empty, with tables set.
2. The harvest in annexed lands is the best in years, even where the people are gone.
3. A Handmaiden-Governor's agent wears a pale shard on a chain. It is cold to the touch.
4. Meeting Vashtera: she wins without effort, spares them, and says nothing about the source of her power.

## Clues, adventure 2 (party level 3 to 5)

1. The party recognises the shard pattern and finds the same mark on the pale crown in old paintings.
2. A defector or hidden record from the Motherhouse names "the price of the first crown" and the year of the vanishing.
3. Near the scar, spirits of the missing are stuck and cannot move on. A Doorkeeper of Velenna can confirm they never arrived.
4. The party learns the shards and the Hollow Crown are connected, and that cutting the shards weakens her.

## The weakness (for the level-5 fight)

Every crown shard the party destroys or removes before the fight takes something from her: with all shards cut she loses Legendary Resistance and Stolen Life (see her stat block). This gives the party a way to prepare and a reason to explore.
