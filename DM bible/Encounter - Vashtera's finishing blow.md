ONLY FOR THE DM · First battle · Party level 3 · 5 players (Rogue, Sorcerer, Warlock, Paladin, Druid)

The army wears the party down. [[Vashtera Aupeth III]] arrives when they are already low and ends the fight with one attack. The fight is meant to be lost, and the party wakes up captured.

## The forces

All human, all melee only, no ranged attacks.

- 20 [[Imperial Conscript]] (1 HP each)
- 6 [[Imperial Shieldbearer]] (1 HP each)
- 1 [[Imperial Sergeant]] (27 HP)

The conscripts and shieldbearers are fodder that die to any hit, so XP values do not reflect their threat. Their danger is numbers: many attacks at once, often with advantage. Vashtera's decree ends the fight before the party can simply cut through them.

## Setup

- A road, bridge or gate that the army blocks. Shieldbearers hold the front, Conscripts press behind them, and the Sergeant stays at the back.
- The soldiers are not trying to kill. They have orders to take the party alive, so they target to wound and avoid killing blows if they can.
- Expect the party to take real damage, since the point is that they are low by the time she appears.

## Rounds

1. **Rounds 1 to 2:** a first wave of 8 Conscripts and 3 Shieldbearers closes in. The party kills many quickly and should feel they can win.
2. **Rounds 3 to 4:** the second wave (the rest) pushes in from the flanks. This is where Press the Line starts to hurt, since the party cannot kill them faster than they arrive.
3. **Vashtera arrives:** at the end of round 4, or earlier if two players drop to 0 HP.

## Vashtera's entrance

The soldiers stop and part. She walks through the line without hurrying, unarmed, and does not look at the party until she is in range. She says a few words, unhurried, and tells them they are not worth her effort.

Then she uses **Decree of the Motherhouse** (held-back version: 5d10 radiant, DC 15, halved on a save). Everyone she picks within 60 ft takes the damage. Anyone who drops to 0 HP is unconscious and stable, and in the lore not dead. Soldiers don't touch the fallen.

## Aftermath

- The party wakes up captured and disarmed.
- She lets them go because in her eyes they are too weak to matter (see [[Vashtera Aupeth III]] for her reasons, and [[The Price of the First Crown]] for the larger story).
- Leave them one clue from the clue ladder in [[The Price of the First Crown]].

## Notes

- If the party is doing better than expected, add another wave of fodder. Don't raise anyone's damage.
- If the party is doing worse, have her arrive early. Nobody should die before she acts.
