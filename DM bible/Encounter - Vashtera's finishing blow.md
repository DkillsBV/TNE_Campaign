ONLY FOR THE DM · First battle · Party level 3 · 5 players (Rogue, Sorcerer, Warlock, Paladin, Druid)

The army wears the party down. [[Vashtera Aupeth III]] arrives when they are already low and ends the fight with one attack. The fight is meant to be lost, and the party wakes up captured.

## Where it happens

The southern border between [[Unton]] and the [[Empire of Am Aupeth]], at the bottom of Dravethis. The battle is fought at the border itself, in the open ground in front of Dunmarch Hold. The party is in **Dunmarch Hold**, a fortified border settlement on the Unton side, gathering information before the fight. (Name is my proposal, change it if you like.) The scar at the heart of the empire is within reach to the north-west, which fits the clues in [[The Price of the First Crown]].

## The battle at large

- **Am Aupeth:** about 2,500 soldiers cross the border. [[Vashtera Aupeth III]] is their general and leads them directly, with no other commander above the four Sergeants' groups. She does not fight until the moment she arrives.
- **Unton:** about 2,500 soldiers from the Dunmarch Hold garrison and nearby holds, roughly equal in numbers, and much more experienced. [[Commander Torgrim Ashvein]] commands them. He does not fight, and the party talks to him before and after. They have never faced Vashtera in person. They have fought Am Aupeth's armies several times and have tried many different tactics against them. Describe their line as practised, not desperate.
- **The party:** they are not the center of the war. They fight one sector of it: the 100 soldiers listed below. The rest of the battle goes on around them, and the party sees pieces of it.
- Without Vashtera, the veteran Unton line would hold or win. Her arrival is what turns the battle.

## Before the battle: the settlement

- The party spends time in the settlement gathering information. Use the adventure 1 clues from [[The Price of the First Crown]] as rumours: empty border villages with tables still set, good harvests in annexed land, officials who speak of the Empress as if her word were already law.
- Unton is arming against Am Aupeth (see [[Unton]]), so locals will be nervous, and refugees from the lost western province can tell stories.
- Dunmarch Hold has a large garrison of Unton soldiers, mostly dwarves, with human levies and a few orcs in the frontier regiments.
- The garrison fights. The army crosses the border and the Unton soldiers hold the line against it, so the party fights alongside them, not alone. Unton's veterans handle most of the army and take real losses, which keeps the 100 enemies in the party's sector from being joined by the rest.
- The garrison is the equal of the army in numbers and better in experience, so it holds its line well until Vashtera arrives.
- The army arrives on the road leading to the settlement. Decide whether the party has warning or is caught off guard.

## The forces

All human, all melee only, no ranged attacks. About 100 soldiers on the battlefield in total:

- 70 [[Imperial Conscript]] (1 HP each)
- 25 [[Imperial Shieldbearer]] (1 HP each)
- 4 [[Imperial Sergeant]] (27 HP each, one per group of about 25)

The conscripts and shieldbearers are fodder that die to any hit, so XP values do not reflect their threat. Their danger is numbers: many attacks at once, often with advantage.

## Setup

- A battlefield at the border in front of the gate, with a road, a ditch or a low wall running across it. Shieldbearers hold the front, Conscripts press behind and around them, and the Sergeants stay at the back of their groups.
- Not all 100 are in contact at once. Only a limited number can reach the party, because they need room to stand. Keep about 12 to 16 soldiers in contact with the party at any time, and as each one dies, a new one steps in. The garrison holds the rest of the army back, so the sea of soldiers around the party is a battle line, with Unton soldiers fighting in it.
- The soldiers are not trying to kill. They have orders to take the party alive, so they target to wound and avoid killing blows if they can.
- Expect the party to take real damage, since the point is that they are low by the time she appears.

## Running 100 soldiers

- Track the army in groups, not one by one. Keep a tally of how many are left in each group.
- Don't roll for every soldier. A Conscript attacking with advantage against AC 14 hits about 70% of the time for an average of 4.5, so call it about 3 damage per Conscript per round. Roll a few of them for the party's feel, and count the rest as an average. Use real rolls for Sergeants.
- Replace the dead at the start of each round: about 4 to 6 new soldiers step into the gaps.

## Rounds

1. **Rounds 1 to 2:** the first group closes in. The party kills many quickly and should feel they can win.
2. **Rounds 3 to 4:** a second group pushes in from the flanks. Press the Line starts to hurt, since the party cannot kill them faster than they arrive. Show the party the Unton line fighting and slowly giving ground.
3. **Vashtera arrives:** at the end of round 4 or 5, or earlier if two players drop to 0 HP.

## Vashtera's entrance

The imperial soldiers stop and part. She walks through the line without hurrying, unarmed, and does not look at the party until she is in range. The Unton soldiers nearby hesitate when they see her. She says a few words, unhurried, and tells the party they are not worth her effort.

Then she uses **Decree of the Motherhouse** (first-battle version: no roll and no save, instant knockout). She picks only the party. Anyone she hits is at 0 HP and unconscious, rolling death saves, and in the lore not dead (her priests stabilise anyone who would die). Soldiers don't touch the fallen. The experienced Unton line, which had held until now, breaks and pulls back toward the walls of Dunmarch Hold, because there is no standing against her.

## Aftermath

- The party wakes up captured and disarmed.
- She lets them go because in her eyes they are too weak to matter (see [[Vashtera Aupeth III]] for her reasons, and [[The Price of the First Crown]] for the larger story).
- Leave them one clue from the clue ladder in [[The Price of the First Crown]].

## Notes

- If the party is doing better than expected, raise the number in contact, not anyone's damage.
- If the party is doing worse, have her arrive early. Nobody should die before she acts.
