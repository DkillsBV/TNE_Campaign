Empress of [[Empire of Am Aupeth]] · BBEG · Cleric-like · Homebrew (new enemy)

Party: 5 players (Rogue, Sorcerer, Warlock, Paladin, Druid), first-timers. They are level 3 when she defeats them in the first battle and level 5 when they defeat her later.

## First battle (party level 3): one attack

She appears when the party is already low on HP. Her single attack is meant to end the fight in one shot.

**Decree of the Motherhouse** · Action · no attack roll
- Every creature of her choice within 60 ft of her takes 5d10 radiant damage (average 27). A DEX save (DC 15) halves it (average 14).
- A creature reduced to 0 HP by this attack is knocked unconscious and stable. It does not roll death saves and does not die. She wants them captured, not dead.

She uses it on her first turn. The fight is meant to be lost, so the party wakes up captured.

## Later fight (party level 5)

The same attack should not look overpowered against a level-5 party, and it doesn't: a full-HP level-5 character has about 40 HP, so 27 damage (14 on a save) leaves them standing. Keep it as one of her normal actions. Only a character who is already low goes down.

## Notes

- Numbers: a level-3 character has about 24 HP, so anyone already hurt drops even on a successful save. A fresh level-5 character survives a failed save.
- If you want it to kill instead of capture, remove the "stable" line.
- Save DC 15 is based on proficiency +3 and Wisdom +4. Hit points, AC, spells and the rest of her stat block are not written yet. They come when you ask for the full BBEG.
- Radiant fits her priesthood role in the [[Empire of Am Aupeth]]. Swap the damage type if her faith ends up tied to one of [[The First]]'s twelve seats.
