Empress of [[Empire of Am Aupeth]] · BBEG · Cleric-like · Homebrew (new enemy)

Party: 5 players (Rogue, Sorcerer, Warlock, Paladin, Druid), first-timers. They are level 3 when she defeats them in the first battle and level 5 when they defeat her later.

## First battle (party level 3): one attack

She appears when the party is already low on HP. Her single attack is meant to end the fight in one shot.

**Decree of the Motherhouse** · Action · no attack roll
- Every creature she chooses within 60 ft is instantly knocked out. There is no damage roll and no save. This is the [[Massive damage]] rule at its extreme: she is far beyond the half-HP threshold.
- A creature knocked out this way is at 0 HP and unconscious, and rolls death saving throws as normal. In the lore it is not dead: the Empress spares them on purpose, and if a character would die, her priests stabilise them, so the party wakes up captured. This is my suggestion for keeping everyone alive, so change it if you want deaths to be real.
- This is the first-battle version only. The level-5 fight uses the full damage version in her stat block.

She uses it on her first turn. The fight is meant to be lost.

## Why she spares them

She captures the party and lets them go because, in her eyes, they are too weak to matter. She is not afraid of them and has no use for them. She holds back on purpose in this fight and treats it as a lesson, not a battle. Compare it to Frieza in Dragon Ball Z: overwhelming, polite, and sure of herself. That arrogance is what the party can use against her later.

What the party learns: she is far stronger than they are, she knows who they are, and she lets them walk away.

## Later fight (party level 5): full stat block

Homebrew. Built for 5 level-5 players as a solo boss, roughly CR 9. Her power comes from [[Vaskor, the Stolen Crown]]; see [[The Price of the First Crown]].

**Vashtera Aupeth III, Empress of Am Aupeth**
Medium Humanoid (Human), Lawful Evil

- **AC** 18 (ceremonial plate, shield of the Motherhouse)
- **HP** 150 (20d8 + 60)
- **Speed** 30 ft
- **Initiative** +1

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 20 (+5) | 18 (+4) |

- **Saving Throws** CON +7, WIS +9, CHA +8
- **Skills** Insight +9, Persuasion +8, Religion +6
- **Damage Resistances** necrotic
- **Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened
- **Senses** passive Perception 15
- **Languages** Common, plus two others of your choice
- **CR** 9 (5,000 XP) · **Proficiency Bonus** +4

**Traits**
- **Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If she fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
- **Stolen Life.** When a creature within 60 ft of her drops to 0 HP, she regains 10 HP.
- **Spellcasting.** Wisdom, spell save DC 17, +9 to hit with spell attacks.
  - At will: *guidance*, *sacred flame*
  - 3/day each: *spirit guardians*, *bestow curse*, *dispel magic*

**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** She makes two Sanctified Scepter attacks.
- **Sanctified Scepter.** Melee Attack Roll: +9, reach 5 ft. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) radiant damage.
- **Decree of the Motherhouse (Recharge 5-6).** Each creature of her choice within 60 ft makes a DC 15 Dexterity save with advantage, taking 33 (6d10) radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. A creature knocked out by it rolls death saving throws with advantage.

**Legendary Actions** (3 per round, one at the end of another creature's turn)
- **Sacred Flame.** She casts *sacred flame* (1 action).
- **Stolen Pull.** One creature within 30 ft makes a DC 17 Constitution save or takes 10 (3d6) necrotic damage and she regains the same number of HP (2 actions).
- **Move.** She moves up to half her speed without provoking opportunity attacks (1 action).

**First battle (level 3).** She uses the instant-knockout version of Decree described above. It needs no roll because the party is already low and she does not take the fight seriously.

## Notes

- Check against [[Massive damage]]: her level-5 Decree deals 6d10 (average 33). A healthy level-5 character has about 40 HP, so half is about 20, and a failed save will usually knock them out instantly. A successful save (average 16) usually does not. This is intended: if her Decree can cause massive damage, it can.
- If you want it to kill instead of capture, remove the line about her priests stabilising anyone who would die.
- Radiant fits her priesthood role in the [[Empire of Am Aupeth]]. Swap the damage type if her faith ends up tied to one of [[The First]]'s twelve seats.
