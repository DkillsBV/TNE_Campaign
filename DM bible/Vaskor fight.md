ONLY FOR THE DM · [[Vaskor, the Stolen Crown]] · Fought in adventure 2 · Homebrew (new enemy)

Party: 5 players, around level 10 (give or take). Built as a solo boss, roughly CR 15. Check the party's real level before using it.

## The setup

He is not free. The party fights a **fragment of him** pushing through the weakening Dravethis seal, inside a ring of runes at the scar in [[Empire of Am Aupeth]]. This keeps him dangerous and beatable without killing a being the Senate needed five seals to hold. See [[The Price of the First Crown]] for his link to [[Vashtera Aupeth III]].

Two ways to win:
1. **Re-bind him:** at the end of each round, a character can use an action to make a DC 17 Arcana or Religion check on the seal. Four successes in total bind the fragment again.
2. **Break the fragment:** reduce him to 0 HP and the fragment collapses. The seal is still weakened afterwards, so this delays the problem and does not solve it.

## Stat block

**Vaskor, Fragment of the Stolen Crown**
Medium Fiend (origin unknown), Lawful Evil

- **AC** 18 (bone-white plate, pale as the crown)
- **HP** 285 (30d8 + 150)
- **Speed** 30 ft, but he cannot leave the 60-ft seal ring

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 (+5) | 14 (+2) | 20 (+5) | 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 22 (+6) |

- **Saving Throws** CON +10, WIS +8, CHA +11
- **Damage Resistances** necrotic, psychic
- **Condition Immunities** charmed, frightened
- **Senses** darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 13
- **Languages** all, telepathy 60 ft
- **CR** 15 (13,000 XP) · **Proficiency Bonus** +5

**Traits**
- **Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If he fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
- **Empty Settings.** Radiant light pours out of the empty jewel settings on his crown. Creatures of his choice within 10 ft of him have disadvantage on saving throws against his Dominion.

**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** He makes two Pried Edge attacks.
- **Pried Edge.** Melee Attack Roll: +11, reach 5 ft. Hit: 15 (1d10 + 10) slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) necrotic damage.
- **Dominion (Recharge 5-6).** Each creature of his choice within 30 ft makes a DC 19 Wisdom save. On a failure, the creature kneels: it is prone and can't make attacks until the end of its next turn.

**Legendary Actions** (3 per round, one at the end of another creature's turn)
- **Enforce Order (1 action).** One creature within 60 ft makes a DC 19 Wisdom save or has speed 0 and can't take reactions until the start of its next turn.
- **Empty Setting (2 actions).** A 30-ft cone of radiant light. Each creature in it makes a DC 19 Dexterity save, taking 36 (8d8) radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.

## Notes

- Dominion and Enforce Order take away the party's choices without dealing damage, so they read as "enforced order" and feel unfair only if overused. Use Dominion on the first round it recharges, then let the seal checks matter.
- If the party is low on HP, think before using Dominion plus two Pried Edge attacks on the same turn.
