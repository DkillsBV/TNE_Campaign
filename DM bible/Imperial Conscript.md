Human army of the [[Empire of Am Aupeth]] · Minion · Melee only · Homebrew (new enemy)

Low HP, low damage, many of them. Use them in large groups. They have no ranged attacks.

**Imperial Conscript**
Medium Humanoid (Human), Lawful Neutral

- **AC** 12 (padded gambeson)
- **HP** 9 (2d8)
- **Speed** 30 ft

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 11 (+0) | 9 (-1) | 10 (+0) | 8 (-1) |

- **Senses** passive Perception 10
- **Languages** Common
- **CR** 1/8 (25 XP) · **Proficiency Bonus** +2

**Traits**
- **Press the Line.** The Conscript has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the Conscript's allies is within 5 ft of that creature and the ally is not incapacitated.

**Actions**
- **Cudgel.** Melee Attack Roll: +3, reach 5 ft. Hit: 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage.

## Notes

- Better in groups of 8 to 12 than in ones and twos. Alone they are not a threat.
- They were drafted, not trained, so they break easily: if the Sergeant ([[Imperial Sergeant]]) is killed, they may flee.
