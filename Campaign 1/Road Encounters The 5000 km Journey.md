Session 2 · Level 3 party · Horse carriage on stone-brick roads

## How to roll

Roll a **d6 for the category** and a **d8 for the encounter**. That gives 48 outcomes. Roll once per leg of the journey, about 5 or 6 times in total. After each encounter, roll a d6: on a 6, the encounter carries a side quest hook (see the end of this document).

Combat-heavy encounters are built around CR 5-6. Size every fight for High difficulty, since a failed approach is the likely outcome.

## 1. Combat (CR 5-6)

1. Wyvern hunting the horses (CR 6)
2. Troll under a stone bridge (CR 5)
3. Highwaymen led by a mage (Mage CR 6, plus bandits)
4. Bulette bursting up through the road (CR 5)
5. Chimera diving from the cliffs (CR 6)
6. Hill giant demanding a toll (CR 5)
7. Gorgon stampeding down the road (CR 5)
8. Revenant hunting one of the passengers or an NPC they carry (CR 5)

## 2. Social

1. Corrupt checkpoint wanting a bribe
2. Noble convoy that offers to hire them as guards
3. Rival carriage racing for the last room at an inn
4. Tax collector inspecting the cargo for contraband
5. Pilgrims begging for a ride
6. Town market where rumours and favours are traded
7. Travelling magistrate accuses the coachman of a crime, so they must argue the case or bribe him
8. Rival adventuring party offers a wager, or a joint job with a split reward

## 3. Hazards and obstacles

1. Flooded road with a dangerous ford
2. Collapsed bridge over a gorge
3. Rockslide blocking a mountain pass
4. Blizzard or thick fog, and they lose the road
5. Broken axle in the middle of nowhere
6. Poisoned water trough that makes the horses sick
7. Sinkhole opens in the road above old tunnels, and the carriage hangs on the edge
8. Wildfire races across the dry fields toward the road, and the horses panic

## 4. Mystery

1. Abandoned village with the tables still set
2. Murder at a roadside inn
3. Milestones that seem to loop the road in circles (fey)
4. Passenger who isn’t who they claim (doppelganger)
5. Empty caravan with signs of a struggle but no bodies
6. Strange symbol carved into a milestone at every leg (the recurring clue)
7. Roadside shrine where the offerings keep vanishing (fey or ghost)
8. An abandoned carriage identical to theirs, with their names on the passenger list

## 5. Stealth and ambush

1. Ogre camp with a sleeping hill giant chief (CR 5)
2. Bandit lookout tower held by a gladiator (CR 5)
3. Smugglers moving goods at night
4. Cultists performing a ritual by the roadside
5. Night patrol led by a vampire spawn (CR 5)
6. Sleeping young white dragon in a ruined waystation (CR 6)
7. Annis hag’s hut by the road, with the hag asleep or away (CR 6)
8. Slaver camp with drunk guards and a cambion overseer (CR 5)

## 6. Wonders and roleplay

1. Ancient waystation with inscriptions from the road builders
2. Giant statue at a crossroads with a hidden secret
3. Travelling merchant selling odd magic items
4. Bard at a campfire sharing stories and rumours
5. Fey crossroads where you trade a memory for a favour
6. Treant or friendly giant guarding a stretch of road who asks for help
7. Harvest fair in a town, with contests, games and rumours, so it works as a rest day
8. Healing spring at a wayside shrine where each drinker must answer one honest question

## Side quest hooks

When the side quest roll hits, use the hook that matches the category you rolled. Offer it as an optional detour, and keep it to 1 or 2 per session. Keep any fights in side quests at CR 2-4, or make them stealth-friendly.

1. **Combat:** A village offers a bounty to clear the wyvern’s or chimera’s nest, a day’s detour off the road. The nest holds 1 CR 5-6 creature or its young.
2. **Social:** A merchant, noble or tax collector’s rival offers gold to deliver a sealed package, or to quietly expose a corrupt official at the next checkpoint.
3. **Hazards:** A stranded caravan needs help getting across a broken bridge or flooded ford. The reward is rare cargo or a free ride on their next leg.
4. **Mystery:** The symbol on the milestones leads to a hidden shrine or cellar a few hours off the road, tying the side quest to the main thread.
5. **Stealth:** Among the bandits or smugglers they ambushed, a prisoner or a stolen shipment turns up, with a map to the leader’s hideout.
6. **Wonders:** The merchant, bard or treant asks for an errand, such as delivering an item to someone in the next city, in return for a favour or a magic item.
