Session 2 · Level 3 party · Horse carriage on stone-brick roads

## How to roll

Roll a **d6 for the category** and a **d8 for the encounter**. That gives 48 outcomes. Roll once per leg of the journey, about 5 or 6 times in total. A 7 or 8 on the d8 is always a side quest, marked [Side quest] in the lists and described at the end of this document.

Combat-heavy encounters are built around CR 5-6. Size every fight for High difficulty, since a failed approach is the likely outcome.

## 1. Combat (CR 5-6)

1. Wyvern hunting the horses (CR 6)
2. Troll under a stone bridge (CR 5)
3. Bulette bursting up through the road (CR 5)
4. Chimera diving from the cliffs (CR 6)
5. Hill giant demanding a toll (CR 5)
6. Gorgon stampeding down the road (CR 5)
7. Highwaymen led by a mage (Mage CR 6, plus bandits) [Side quest]
8. Revenant hunting one of the passengers or an NPC they carry (CR 5) [Side quest]

## 2. Social

1. Corrupt checkpoint wanting a bribe
2. Rival carriage racing for the last room at an inn
3. Tax collector inspecting the cargo for contraband
4. Pilgrims begging for a ride
5. Town market where rumours and favours are traded
6. Travelling magistrate accuses the coachman of a crime, so they must argue the case or bribe him
7. Noble convoy that offers to hire them as guards [Side quest]
8. Rival adventuring party offers a wager, or a joint job with a split reward [Side quest]

## 3. Hazards and obstacles

1. Flooded road with a dangerous ford
2. Rockslide blocking a mountain pass
3. Blizzard or thick fog, and they lose the road
4. Broken axle in the middle of nowhere
5. Poisoned water trough that makes the horses sick
6. Wildfire races across the dry fields toward the road, and the horses panic
7. Collapsed bridge over a gorge [Side quest]
8. Sinkhole opens in the road above old tunnels, and the carriage hangs on the edge [Side quest]

## 4. Mystery

1. Abandoned village with the tables still set
2. Milestones that seem to loop the road in circles (fey)
3. Passenger who isn’t who they claim (doppelganger)
4. Strange symbol carved into a milestone at every leg (the recurring clue)
5. Roadside shrine where the offerings keep vanishing (fey or ghost)
6. An abandoned carriage identical to theirs, with their names on the passenger list
7. Murder at a roadside inn [Side quest]
8. Empty caravan with signs of a struggle but no bodies [Side quest]

## 5. Stealth and ambush

1. Ogre camp with a sleeping hill giant chief (CR 5)
2. Bandit lookout tower held by a gladiator (CR 5)
3. Smugglers moving goods at night
4. Night patrol led by a vampire spawn (CR 5)
5. Sleeping young white dragon in a ruined waystation (CR 6)
6. Annis hag’s hut by the road, with the hag asleep or away (CR 6)
7. Cultists performing a ritual by the roadside [Side quest]
8. Slaver camp with drunk guards and a cambion overseer (CR 5) [Side quest]

## 6. Wonders and roleplay

1. Ancient waystation with inscriptions from the road builders
2. Giant statue at a crossroads with a hidden secret
3. Bard at a campfire sharing stories and rumours
4. Fey crossroads where you trade a memory for a favour
5. Harvest fair in a town, with contests, games and rumours, so it works as a rest day
6. Healing spring at a wayside shrine where each drinker must answer one honest question
7. Travelling merchant selling odd magic items [Side quest]
8. Treant or friendly giant guarding a stretch of road who asks for help [Side quest]

## Side quest details

Each quest is an optional detour of about a day. Fights stay at CR 2-4 or can be solved with stealth, and every quest can feed the recurring symbol clue if you want it to.

**Combat 7, Highwaymen led by a mage.** The mage carries a ledger and a wanted poster for a local lord’s missing heir. The quest is to follow the ledger to the gang’s hideout in a cave an hour off the road and recover the loot. It holds 3-4 bandits and a scout (CR 1/2 to 1), a locked chest, and a note naming who hired the gang. Reward: the lord’s bounty and a minor magic item.

**Combat 8, Revenant.** The revenant hunts a passenger over a murder years ago, and the passenger swears they are innocent. The quest is to find the real killer before the revenant strikes again, starting in a village off the road with a witness and a forged confession. It holds an investigation, a scene with the village elder, and a final confrontation with the real killer, a spy or knight (CR 1-3). Reward: the revenant stands down and owes a favour, and the killer’s hoard.

**Social 7, Noble convoy.** A noble hires them to guard the convoy for one leg. The quest is to get a sealed chest to the next town, though the chest is the real target. It holds an ambush by 4-6 thieves (CR 1/2 to 1) halfway through, a traitor among the drivers, and a letter in the chest that reveals a court plot. Reward: gold and a letter of passage that skips checkpoints.

**Social 8, Rival adventuring party.** The rivals propose splitting the reward for clearing a ruined toll house of undead, and they plan to double-cross the party at the end. It holds 2-3 ghouls or a ghast (CR 1-2), a trapped vault, and the backstab. Reward: a share of the loot, or all of it if they outplay the rivals.

**Hazards 7, Collapsed bridge.** A stranded caravan is stuck on the far side. The quest is to find another way across, such as a narrow gorge path or a rebuilt crossing. It holds a skill challenge (Athletics, Survival, carpentry), a small goblin or giant spider nest on the gorge path (CR 1/4 to 1), and 2-3 stranded NPCs. Reward: rare cargo and a free ride on their next leg.

**Hazards 8, Sinkhole.** The road collapsed into old tunnels built by the road’s original builders. The quest is to climb down, rescue two trapped workers, and find out why the tunnels failed. It holds cave-ins, 2 giant centipedes or an ankheg (CR 1/4 to 2), and a sealed vault carved with the recurring symbol. Reward: an ancient road-builder relic and a clue for the main thread.

**Mystery 7, Murder at a roadside inn.** The innkeeper has a body and five suspects: a merchant, a bard, a guard, a pilgrim and the innkeeper’s son. The killer plans to leave at dawn. It holds clues in the rooms, a hidden motive, and a confrontation with the killer, who may be a doppelganger (CR 2). Reward: free rooms for the rest of the journey and a tip about the road ahead.

**Mystery 8, Empty caravan.** Tracks lead away from the wagons to a hidden cellar or cave. It holds a tracking scene, one surviving prisoner, and 4-6 captors with a leader (CR 1/2 to 2), which can be done by stealth. The crates carry the recurring symbol. Reward: the rescued merchants’ goods and a clue about the symbol.

**Stealth 7, Cultists’ ritual.** The ritual is only half finished. The quest is to sabotage it before midnight, or to follow the cult leader to a farmhouse. It holds 6 cultists (CR 1/8 to 1/2), a cult fanatic leader (CR 2), a ritual circle to disrupt, and a half-summoned imp or quasit (CR 1) if they fail. Reward: a cult tome showing the recurring symbol and pointing to the next lead.

**Stealth 8, Slaver camp.** Eight to ten prisoners sit in cages, while the cambion overseer is the main encounter. The quest is to free them and follow the slaver’s ledger to the buyer in the next town. It holds a key prisoner (a healer or scout), the ledger, and a camp that suits stealth. Reward: the grateful NPC joins the carriage for one leg or owes a favour, and the ledger names the buyers.

**Wonders 7, Travelling merchant.** He asks them to deliver a package to someone in the next city and pays on delivery. The package hums or whispers. It holds a thief or rival tailing them (CR 1/2 to 2) and a reveal on delivery, which shows the package is a stolen relic or a key. Reward: a magic item or discount and a letter of introduction.

**Wonders 8, Treant or friendly giant.** The guardian asks them to stop poachers who are felling the old trees along its stretch of road. It holds a short hunt, 4-5 poachers (CR 1/8 to 1/2), and an angry owlbear they have wounded (CR 3). Reward: safe passage along that road and a boon, such as healing herbs, a potion or a favour.
