## **Children of the Old War**

The **Ashborn** are among the rarest people in Navaerith. They have no kingdom of their own and are scattered throughout the continent, often living quietly among Dragonborn or Elves.

Their bloodline began during the ancient demon war, when some Dragonborn and Elves were exposed to demonic corruption. Generations later, their descendants began to show unusual traits and became known as the Ashborn.

Because of their appearance and heritage, many Dragonborn distrust them, believing they are somehow connected to the demons.

The Ashborn themselves know there is some truth to that fear—but not in the way most believe.

They inherited a strange sensitivity to demonic presence. They can sometimes feel when something unnatural is nearby, or when the ancient magic beneath Navaerith is disturbed.

For this reason, most Ashborn keep their abilities hidden and avoid drawing attention to themselves.

They are not guardians.

They are not an organized order.

They are simply survivors of an ancient legacy that refuses to disappear.

And now, after generations of silence, **the Ashborn are beginning to feel something stirring beneath the land once again.**
