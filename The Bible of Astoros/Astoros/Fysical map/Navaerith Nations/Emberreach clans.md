### _The Heart of Navaerith_

Emberreach became the political heart of Navaerith because it was one of the earliest places where **Dragonborn and Elves truly built a society together**.

Dragonborn settlements spread across the plains and rivers, while Elven communities grew around the ancient forests. Over time, their cultures became intertwined, and both peoples helped shape the region.

At its center stands **Vaerath-Kor**, a great Dragonborn city where Elven families have lived for generations. Their old architecture and traditions remain woven into the city alongside the Dragonborn clans.

After centuries of clan conflicts, the **Ember Council** was formed to keep peace between the different powers of Navaerith.

Beneath the city, however, burns the ancient **First Flame**. Most see it as a symbol of unity and hope. Only a few know that it is connected to the ancient magic protecting Navaerith.

Now the flame is beginning to weaken.
