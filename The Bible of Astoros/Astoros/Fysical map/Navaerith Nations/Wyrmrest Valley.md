## The Valley of Ancient Wings

Wyrmrest Valley is one of the oldest and most untouched regions of Navaerith. Its enormous forests and rivers are home to **Dragonborn, Elves, and dragons**, who have shared the valley for generations.

The Elves came here long ago after losing their original homeland, finding refuge among the ancient forests. The dragons had already lived there for centuries, and although the relationship between them was never simple, the two eventually learned to coexist. Dragonborn settlements grew around the edges of the forests, becoming an important part of the valley as well.

At the heart of Wyrmrest stands **Wyrmheart**, an enormous tree said to have grown from one of Vaerith's scales. The Elves protect it, the Dragonborn respect it, and the dragons remember its true importance.

Recently, however, Wyrmheart has begun to wither. Black leaves have appeared among its branches, while some of the oldest dragons have begun leaving the valley.

Something is changing in Wyrmrest.
