Ashenstep was once a fertile land filled with grasslands and rivers.

Then, thousands of years ago, it became a wasteland.

Nobody knows exactly what happened.

Buried beneath the sand are ancient ruins and enormous black stones known as **the Devil's Teeth**.

Most Dragonborn believe they are remnants of the ancient demon war.

The few Ashborn who have visited the region claim something different.

They believe the stones aren't ruins.

**They're doors.**
