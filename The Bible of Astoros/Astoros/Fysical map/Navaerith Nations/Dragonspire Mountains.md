### _Formerly Vael'Tharin_

Before the mountains existed, this region was known as **Vael'Tharin**, a beautiful and fertile land dominated by the legendary **Crimson Forest**.

The forest was unlike anything else in Navaerith. Towering trees covered the land, their bark and leaves glowing a brilliant crimson. Their unusual color came from the enormous rivers of lava flowing beneath the earth, which provided the forest with heat and magical energy.

Vael'Tharin was home to several Dragonborn clans and a small Elven population. The two races lived alongside one another, with the Dragonborn protecting the forest and the Elves tending to its ancient magic.

Then the demons attacked.

The battle devastated the entire region. The earth split open, volcanoes erupted, and the lava beneath the forest surged to the surface.

The Crimson Forest burned.

Most of the Dragonborn inhabitants were killed, while the surviving Elves **fled east toward the ancient forests of Wyrmrest Valley**.

The destruction changed the land forever.

When the earth finally settled, the once-flat region had become a gigantic mountain range.

**The Dragonspire Mountains.**

But not everyone who survived remained unchanged.

Some Dragonborn were exposed to the demonic corruption left behind by the invasion. Their bodies survived, but their once-bright crimson eyes slowly lost their light.

They became known as:

### **The Hollow-Eyed.**

Some believe they are cursed.

Some believe they are merely scarred survivors.

And others whisper that something from the demons still lives inside them.
