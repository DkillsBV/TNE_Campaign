
## **A Land Bound by Ancient Magic**

Navaerith is an ancient continent shaped by the influence of **Dragonborn, Elves, and dragons**. For thousands of years, these peoples built their homes across its forests, mountains, rivers, and plains, creating countless clans, kingdoms, and traditions.

Yet beneath the land lies a secret older than any civilization. Long ago, powerful demons were imprisoned deep beneath Navaerith, and the continent itself became part of their prison. Ancient magic was woven into the land to keep them sealed away, while the great dragon **Vaerith the First Flame** became the guardian of that ancient power.

As generations passed, the true purpose of the magic was forgotten. Kingdoms rose and fell, clans fought and reunited, and the different peoples of Navaerith developed their own cultures and histories.

But the old prison has never stopped weakening.

Strange events are beginning to appear across the continent, and ancient powers are stirring once again. The people of Navaerith may have forgotten the truth of their land, but the land has not forgotten what lies beneath it.

# Species:

## Dragonborn: The Dominant People

Dragonborn are the main inhabitants of Navaerith. Every region has Dragonborn clans, though their cultures differ greatly depending on where they live.

They are the descendants of the ancient peoples who first took responsibility for guarding Navaerith and its secrets.

## Elves: The Refugees of Vael'Tharin

Elves were once closely connected to the **Crimson Forest**, where they protected its ancient magic alongside the Dragonborn.

After the destruction of Vael'Tharin, most of the surviving Elves **fled east into Wyrmrest Valley**, seeking refuge in its ancient forests.

Because their population was already small, Elves remain relatively rare in modern Navaerith. **Wyrmrest is their main stronghold**, although a few Elves can be found elsewhere.

This also means that the Elves of Wyrmrest still remember the Crimson Forest and consider its destruction one of the greatest tragedies in their history.

## Ashborn: The Marked

The Ashborn are an extremely rare group of Tieflings descended from Dragonborn and Elves who were directly exposed to the demonic corruption during the ancient wars.

Most Dragonborn never encounter an Ashborn in their lifetime.

Those who exist are often feared or mistrusted because of their connection to demonic energy. However, Ashborn possess a strange ability to **sense the demons and the weakening of the ancient seals**.

## **Demons:** The Ancient Darkness

The demons are ancient beings that existed long before the kingdoms of Navaerith. They are creatures of **chaos, corruption, and destruction**, once sealed deep beneath the continent after a forgotten war.

Unlike ordinary creatures, demons do not simply seek territory or power. They seek to **break the boundaries between their world and the mortal realm**, turning the lands they reach into places of corruption.

Most people believe the demons are nothing more than an ancient legend.

But the oldest records tell a different story.

**They were imprisoned, not destroyed.**

And now, after thousands of years, their prison is beginning to weaken.
