_The western mountains of Juvessa_

### A Fist, Not a Fortress

Edwarovii holds the western mountains the way a fist holds a rock — not gracefully, but permanently. It is the oldest continuously warring state on Juvessa, and it wears that fact as identity rather than embarrassment. Where its neighbors build treaties, Edwarovii builds campaigns; where they treat peace as something to preserve, Edwarovii has always treated it as a resource to be spent down, deliberately, whenever the treasury and the army both happen to be full at the same time. Ask any ruler on the continent why their nation keeps a standing army at all, and the honest answer, more often than not, is Edwarovii.

Its capital reflects the philosophy outright. Built as a great spiraling fortress-city climbing the western peaks, it was never designed to be lived in peacefully — every ring of the spiral is a chokepoint, every plaza doubles as a marshaling yard, and the royal seat at its center sits behind seven gates specifically because an eighth was judged unnecessary. Foreign envoys call it intimidating. Edwarovii calls it honest.

### A History Measured in Campaigns

Edwarovii's chronicles are not organized by reign, the way most kingdoms record their history — they're organized by campaign. Its royal line has changed more than once, sometimes through the crown simply being taken by whichever general had most recently returned victorious and popular enough to claim it, and the throne has generally been comfortable with that arrangement, since a kingdom built on conquest has never seen much use for a ruler who couldn't win one. The mountains themselves have never been fully pacified even by Edwarovii's own crown — half the honor of commanding its armies has always come from campaigns fought as much against restless holds within its own borders as against the neighboring Kingdom of Bricky or the Wessyri lowlands.

That constant state of low-grade war has, paradoxically, made Edwarovii one of the most stable nations on Juvessa. A populace raised to expect the next campaign rarely revolts over the current one, and a crown that wins wars regularly has never had much trouble justifying its own existence.

### Its People

Edwarovii is predominantly **orcish**, its warrior culture, clan-holds, and mountain fortresses built around generations of conquest rather than settlement — a people who have simply never stopped treating strength as the primary currency worth having. **Humans** make up a significant portion of its lowland levies and logistics corps, absorbed generations ago from campaigns that annexed rather than razed. **Dwarves** run its mountain forges and siege-engineering, turning out the arms and war-machines that have made Edwarovii's campaigns as feared for their engineering as for their numbers. **Goliaths** serve prominently among its champions and shock-troops, drawn by a culture that rewards exactly the kind of strength they're built for. A smaller population of **tieflings and gnomes** round out the kingdom, mostly tacticians, quartermasters, and the occasional mercenary captain who found Edwarovii's blunt meritocracy more honest than anywhere else on the continent.

### The Standing Host

Edwarovii keeps no illusions about its army being purely defensive — the **Standing Host** is built, funded, and maintained explicitly for offense, and everyone in the kingdom knows it. Unlike most nations, which raise armies reluctantly and disband them gladly, Edwarovii's military never truly stands down between campaigns; it simply redirects itself toward whichever internal hold or external border currently needs reminding of who holds the mountains. Command is earned through victory rather than birthright, which has produced a warrior aristocracy almost entirely self-made — and a kingdom that has never had much patience for peace lasting longer than it takes to plan the next war.
