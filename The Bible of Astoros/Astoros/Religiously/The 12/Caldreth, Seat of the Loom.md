**Domain:** Fate, prophecy, the pattern of choices already made  
**Symbol:** A loom with one thread pulled taut and glowing  
**Colors:** Ash-white and deep plum  
**Clergy:** Threadbound (oracles who promise likely patterns, never certainties)  
Caldreth records fate rather than deciding it, weaving choices into a tapestry so vast even she hasn't seen it unrolled in full. Her seat functions as the Senate's long-range advisory office, consulted before major resolutions though never binding.
