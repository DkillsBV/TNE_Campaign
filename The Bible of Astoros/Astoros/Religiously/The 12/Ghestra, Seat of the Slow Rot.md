**Domain:** Plague, decay, the quiet failure of things  
**Symbol:** A wilted flower with a single seed still intact at its center  
**Colors:** Sickly green and bruised yellow  
**Clergy:** Minimal; mostly placated rather than worshipped  
Ghestra unmakes things slowly and completely rather than violently. She reports twice yearly to the Senate on which decays are running their natural course versus being accelerated by interference, and holds an unexpected working relationship with Velenna, both seeing themselves as caretakers of an inevitable process.
