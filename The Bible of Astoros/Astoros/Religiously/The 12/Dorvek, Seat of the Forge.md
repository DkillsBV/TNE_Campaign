**Domain:** Smithing, craft, oaths, creation through labor  
**Symbol:** A hammer crossed with a key  
**Colors:** Iron-grey and ember-orange  
**Clergy:** Answerers  
Dorvek insists nothing worth having survives its first draft. He chairs the Committee on Revisions, which reviews and re-forges old Senate rulings that no longer hold, working closely with Thalwen to keep every revision properly recorded.
