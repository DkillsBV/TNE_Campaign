**Domain:** Chaos, chance, revolution, healthy disruption  
**Symbol:** A wheel with one spoke missing  
**Colors:** Every color at once, deliberately mismatched  
**Clergy:** The Unraveled  
Thiska is the Senate's designated dissenter, keeping the Hall from mistaking tradition for correctness. By ancient charter, no resolution may pass without her having had the chance to object — even the seats who find her exhausting agree it's one of the Senate's wisest rules.
