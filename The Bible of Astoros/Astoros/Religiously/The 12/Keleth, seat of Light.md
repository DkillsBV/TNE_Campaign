**Domain:** Light, truth, sight, honest judgment  
**Symbol:** An unblinking eye inside an open flame  
**Colors:** White and gold  
**Clergy:** Unshutterers (judges, healers, lighthouse-keepers)  
Keleth oversees everything that depends on being seen clearly. His clergy are trained to distinguish _illumination_ from _exposure_ — his portfolio is understanding, not humiliation. He often chairs joint Senate sessions and shares a standing dawn/dusk protocol with Morwenne, plus a long-worked-out agreement with Velenna on judgment versus guidance of the dead.
