**Domain:** War, conflict, the fair proving of causes  
**Symbol:** A broken sword re-forged with a visible seam  
**Colors:** Rust-red and dull bronze  
**Clergy:** Seam-Keepers (truce witnesses, duel officiants)  
Gorath judges _how_ a war is fought, never who's right to fight it, and holds particular contempt for ambush, poison, or slaughtering the unarmed. He co-chairs the Committee on Guardianship with Aurelith and is the hardest vote to win when force is proposed.
