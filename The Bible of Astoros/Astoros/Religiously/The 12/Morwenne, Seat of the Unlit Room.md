**Domain:** Dark, secrecy, grief, the necessary unknown  
**Symbol:** A candle snuffed mid-flicker  
**Colors:** Black and deep violet  
**Clergy:** Curtain-Keepers  
Morwenne governs the half of experience light doesn't reach — the dark that lets seeds germinate, the grief that must be sat with before release. She considers herself light's complement, not its rival, and co-presides the dawn/dusk sessions with Keleth
