**Domain:** Death, transition, the guided dead, rest  
**Symbol:** An open door with no handle on the near side  
**Colors:** Soft grey and pale lantern-blue  
**Clergy:** Doorkeepers (hospice-tenders, funeral officiants, grief counselors)  
Velenna does not kill. Death happens elsewhere — to blade, sickness, age, greed, or chaos — and only then does she arrive to guide the dead onward. She presides unopposed over any Senate matter touching mortality, and her single absolute rule, honored without exception, is that she guides the dead without judgment.
