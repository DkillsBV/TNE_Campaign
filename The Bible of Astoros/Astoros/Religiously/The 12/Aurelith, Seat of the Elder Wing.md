**Domain:** Guardian dragons, protective hoarding, ancient vows  
**Symbol:** A coiled wing wrapped around a small flame  
**Colors:** Deep blue and platinum  
**Clergy:** Her literal dragon children serve as clergy and enforcers  
Aurelith measures wealth by what is protected, not held, and is the Senate's slowest, most patient member. She co-chairs the Committee on Guardianship with Gorath, authorizing divine force only when truly warranted.
