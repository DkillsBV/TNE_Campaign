**Domain:** Feral dragons, conquest, unchecked greed  
**Symbol:** A serpent coiled around its own hoard  
**Colors:** Blood-red and sulfur-yellow  
**Clergy:** None organized; mortals placate rather than worship  
Vryxath represents appetite left unmanaged, and attends the Hall rarely. His seated presence obliges Aurelith's committee to justify every ruling on dragonkind rigorously, since he's quick to object if a resolution leaves feral dragons ungoverned rather than merely unpunished.
