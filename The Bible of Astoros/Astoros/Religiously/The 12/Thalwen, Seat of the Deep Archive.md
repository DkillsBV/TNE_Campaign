**Domain:** Knowledge, memory, scholarship  
**Symbol:** An open book with a lock hanging from its spine, unfastened  
**Colors:** Deep teal and silver  
**Clergy:** Bound Readers  
Thalwen believes every piece of knowledge carries a cost, even a small one, and catalogs disasters as carefully as triumphs. He serves as the Senate's permanent clerk — no ruling is considered final until he enters it into the Ledger of Ages.
