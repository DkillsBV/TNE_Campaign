**The Convocation**

Before the twelve, there was **the First** — a single, formless deity holding every domain at once: light and dark, forge and rot, war and love, life and death, all in one undivided attention. It wasn't cruel, just _singular_ — and a singular thing trying to attend to everything attends to nothing well. Storms broke where it meant to warn. Truths went unspoken because its attention was elsewhere. Nothing was malicious; it simply couldn't be everywhere at once, and mortals suffered for the gaps.

The First noticed its own failure and chose not to keep trying harder alone. Instead, it **divided its labor on purpose**, drawing twelve portions out of itself in sequence, each weighted with will and a portfolio it could tend properly:

1. [[Keleth, seat of Light]] — light and honest judgment, drawn first so what followed could be seen clearly.
2. [[Morwenne, Seat of the Unlit Room]] — dark and grief, drawn at once after, so light would have a partner rather than a rival.
3. [[Dorvek, Seat of the Forge]] — smithing and oaths, given hands to build what the others could only see or guide.
4. [[Aurelith, Seat of the Elder Wing]] — guardian dragons and protective hoarding, set to patiently defend what Dorvek built.
5. [[Vryxath, Seat of the Coiled Hoard]] — feral dragons and greed, drawn reluctantly, so appetite could be named and watched rather than hidden.
6. [[Thiska, Seat of the Loose Thread]] — chaos and disruption, drawn to keep the new order from calcifying into the First's old failure.
7. [[Velenna, Seat of the Gentle Door]] — death, drawn with the most care of all; built _without_ the power to end a life, only the duty to guide a soul once death had already happened elsewhere.
8. [[Caldreth, Seat of the Loom]] — fate, drawn to keep account of every choice, so the First wouldn't forget what the division had been for.
9. [[Gorath, Seat of the Tested Blade]] — war, drawn to share a burden the First had once carried alone: deciding when force was justified.
10. [[Ghestra, Seat of the Slow Rot]] — plague and decay, drawn quietly to hold rot the way Vryxath held hunger — named, not eliminated.
11. [[Isolyn, Seat of the Kept Hearth]] — love and hospitality, drawn last on purpose, insurance that whatever the other eleven disagreed on, love would stay non-negotiable.
12. [[Thalwen, Seat of the Deep Archive]] — knowledge, drawn to remember the whole process, so it would be recorded, not mythologized into vagueness.

What remained of the First became the **[[CONCORD HALL, Seat of the Senate]]**

— a real, physical place where the twelve still meet as equals, belonging to no single seat. This isn't disputed theology; it happened, and the twelve themselves don't deny it. What's lost is only its _location_. No surviving map marks it, no pilgrim route leads there, and the twelve arrive at each equinox without ever describing the way. Whether it was hidden on purpose, buried by time, or simply never needed a fixed address once the First stopped being a single traveling thing, no one now living — mortal or god — can say for certain. What's certain is that it's out there somewhere, and that no mortal power has ever found it to hold leverage over all twelve at once.
