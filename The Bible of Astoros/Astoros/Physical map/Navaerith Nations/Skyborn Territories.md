The Skyborn Territories are vast plains, cliffs, and high plateaus.

The Dragonborn here developed a culture centered around the sky and the ancient dragons. They became explorers, messengers, scholars, and astronomers.

They are one of the most outward-looking Dragonborn cultures in Navaerith and are responsible for maintaining contact between distant clans.

Their astronomers have recently noticed something strange:

Several stars that have existed for thousands of years are disappearing.

And a strange red star has appeared in their place.

They call it:

### **The Watching Eye.**
