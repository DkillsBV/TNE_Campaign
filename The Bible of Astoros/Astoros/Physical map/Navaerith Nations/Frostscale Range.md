Frostscale is almost entirely inhabited by Dragonborn clans.

Its people are famous for their strength, discipline, and warrior traditions.

Deep beneath the glaciers lies the **Frozen Gate**, one of the ancient seals imprisoning the demons beneath Navaerith.

The Frostscale clans have guarded it for generations.

Their oldest law remains:

> **"The Gate must never open."**

Recently, however, cracks have appeared in the ice surrounding the gate.

And some guards claim they can hear something knocking from beneath it.
