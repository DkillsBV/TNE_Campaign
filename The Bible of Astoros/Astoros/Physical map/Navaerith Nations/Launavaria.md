Launavaria is a region of rivers, lakes, wetlands, and coastal settlements.

Its Dragonborn became sailors, merchants, fishermen, and explorers, making Launavaria the main gateway between Navaerith and the outside world.

The region is more culturally diverse than most of Navaerith because of the constant arrival of foreign travelers.

But sailors fear one place above all others:

### **The Silent Waters.**

Ships occasionally disappear there and sometimes return months later completely empty.

Recently, sailors have begun reporting strange lights beneath the ocean.
