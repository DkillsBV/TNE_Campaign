Stonefang is dominated by massive mountains and underground Dragonborn cities.

Its clans are famous for mining, smithing, engineering, and construction.

For centuries they dug deeper into the mountains, searching for valuable minerals and ancient relics.

Eventually, they discovered enormous tunnels that clearly predated Dragonborn civilization.

The tunnels appeared to lead toward the deepest parts of Navaerith.

The elders immediately sealed them.

But miners have recently begun disappearing.

Those who explore the tunnels speak of strange whispers coming from below.
