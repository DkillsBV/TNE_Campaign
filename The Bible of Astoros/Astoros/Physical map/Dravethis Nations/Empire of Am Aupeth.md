_The pale expanse south of Cangansov_

Am Aupeth was once a modest matriarchal kingdom, ruled — as it has been for as long as records survive — by an unbroken line of Empresses descended from the first **Motherhouse**, which declared that only a woman's hand could hold the realm together. Sons of the royal line are honored but constitutionally barred from the throne.

Under the current Empress, **Vashtera Aupeth III**, that quiet kingdom became something else entirely. The Motherhouse began preaching a new doctrine: that the surrounding lands were never truly foreign, only _unreunited_ — territories stolen from Am Aupeth's rightful inheritance generations ago, and that humanity, guided by the Empress's sacred bloodline, was owed their return. Within the last several years, this doctrine has been made real. Neighbor after neighbor has been folded into the empire — through arranged marriages that ended conveniently in a groom's early death, through debt and engineered famine, and through open war fought with a brutality now whispered about as far as Taginumn. State criers call it reunification. Everyone else calls it conquest.

### An Order Built on Blood

Am Aupeth's hierarchy is as rigid as its borders are expanding. **Humans** alone may serve in the priesthood, hold noble title, or sit in the Motherhouse — citizenship itself is treated as a matter of blood purity, tracked and certified generation to generation.

Beneath them, the empire's other peoples are sorted by use. **Halflings and gnomes**, absorbed wholesale from annexed border territories, are tolerated as laborers and clerks under the Handmaiden-Governors, but barred from rank or title — and entire villages have been registered, relocated, or simply erased from the map once a region is declared "pacified." **Orcs** are conscripted as shock troops and border enforcers, prized for their strength in battle and given nothing resembling citizenship in return; official doctrine treats them as a useful tool rather than a people. Worst off are the **tieflings**, blamed in Motherhouse sermons for plagues and failed harvests that predate the empire by centuries — scapegoated, surveilled, and disproportionately among those who vanish near the great scar at the empire's heart.

That scar — a vast circular wound in the land the Motherhouse's own records name only "the price of the first crown" — is where an entire population disappeared in a single terrible year long before Vashtera's reign. Am Aupeth's soil has been unnaturally fertile ever since, and no one in power has ever explained why.

### The Empire Today

Every conquered territory answers to a **Handmaiden-Governor**, loyal only to the Motherhouse. Cooperative nobles are exiled comfortably to the capital as ornaments; those who resist simply vanish from the histories. To diplomats, Am Aupeth is a rising empire offering order and prosperity to those willing to submit. To the halflings, orcs, and tieflings living under its new banners, it is something closer to a machine built to sort the worthy from the disposable — and it is still growing.
