The land of the Dragon-Crowned Peaks!

Vhaerakoth is the northernmost province of Gryndovar, a brutal mountainous land where jagged peaks, deep ravines, and ancient dragon nests dominate the landscape. Vhaerakoth territory is not a place where civilization conquered nature. It is a place where civilization learned to survive alongside it.

## 📜 The History of Vhaerakoth

### I. The Age of the First Flame

Long before the kingdoms of Gyndovar were established, the mountains of Vhaerakoth were home to the first dragons.

The oldest legends claim that the mountains were formed when Vaelrakh, the First Ember, descended from the sky and tore open the earth with its claws. Its blood flowed through the mountains, creating rivers of molten stone and leaving behind the rich red rock that defines the province.

Whether Vaelrakh truly existed or is simply a story passed down through generations, one thing is certain: dragons have ruled these peaks for thousands of years.

Their bones lie beneath the mountains, and their descendants still nest in the highest cliffs.

### II. The Founding of the Mountain Holds

Humans and other mortal peoples began settling in Vhaerakoth roughly 700 years ago.

They were driven there by war, famine, and the collapse of neighboring kingdoms. The mountains offered shelter, but they came with a price.

The first settlers built their homes inside natural caves and carved fortresses directly into the mountainsides. Rather than attempting to exterminate the dragons, the early clans established ancient agreements with certain dragon families.

These agreements became known as The Ember Oaths.

Under the oaths:

- The mountain clans would not hunt dragons or disturb their nesting grounds.
- Dragons would not burn settlements that honored the ancient boundaries.
- In times of great war, the clans could call upon their sworn dragon allies.

Not every dragon accepted the oaths. Many still view mortals as trespassers.

### III. The War of Broken Wings

Two centuries ago, a powerful king attempted to conquer the entire province. He believed that controlling the dragons would give him an army capable of defeating every rival kingdom on Gyndovar.

His armies marched into the mountains, capturing dragon eggs and destroying sacred nesting sites.

The response was catastrophic.

The dragons of Vhaerakoth united for the first time in centuries. They descended upon the invading armies, burning fortresses, collapsing mountain passes, and turning entire valleys into fields of blackened stone.

The king's army was destroyed in a single season.

The war became known as The War of Broken Wings, though the name is disputed. The dragon clans call it The Cleansing of Intruders, while the surviving human clans call it The Betrayal of the Oaths.

The ruined fortresses still remain in the highlands, and some contain dragon eggs that have never hatched.

### The geography

|---|---|
|The Ashen Crown|The highest mountain range, home to the oldest dragons.|
|The Broken Stair|A treacherous chain of mountain passes used by traders and armies.|
|The Ember Valleys|Fertile valleys warmed by volcanic springs, where most settlements exist.|
|The Wyrm's Teeth|Jagged peaks containing ancient dragon caves and lost fortresses.|

### The people

The people of Vhaerakoth are known for their endurance, strict traditions, and distrust of outsiders. They value strength, loyalty, and the ability to survive harsh conditions.

Most settlements are built from dark stone and reinforced with metal. Their smiths are renowned throughout Gyndovar for crafting weapons that can withstand extreme heat.

Some of the most prestigious warriors are Ashbound Knights—fighters who have survived a ritual journey into the dragon mountains and returned with a mark of fire.

Not every Ashbound Knight has a dragon companion. Many consider the title an honor earned through survival alone.

## 🐉 The Central Conflict

The dragons are disappearing.

For generations, the ancient dragons of Vhaerakoth have guarded their nesting grounds. But in recent decades, the number of dragons has begun to decline.

Some have vanished into the deepest mountains. Others have been found dead, their bodies drained of the magical fire that once flowed through their veins.

The mountain clans blame foreign kingdoms. The dragons blame the mortals. And a secretive group known as The Cinder Covenant believes that the only way to save dragonkind is to awaken Vaelrakh, the First Ember, beneath the mountains.
